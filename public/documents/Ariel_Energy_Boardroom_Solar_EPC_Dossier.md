### The SREDA Net-Metering Advantage
Under the Government of Bangladesh's *Net-Metering Guidelines 2018*, commercial and industrial consumers connect approved rooftop solar systems directly to the utility grid via bi-directional smart meters. During daylight hours (9:00 AM – 3:30 PM), solar feeds internal operating loads first. Surplus electricity automatically exports to the grid, earning monetary credits that offset evening or weekend power consumption at the full published tariff rate.
